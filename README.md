One original funny line, written by a small Python program for
[make.fun's "The One-Line Laugh" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f).

## How it works

`main.py` writes a single sentence of at most 200 characters to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is a fresh, everyday observation with its
surprise saved for the end: a fitness tracker celebrating steps that were
mostly spent pacing the kitchen, undecided about dinner.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry

> My smartwatch congratulated me on 10,000 steps today, but 9,000 of them
> were just me pacing the kitchen deciding what to eat.
